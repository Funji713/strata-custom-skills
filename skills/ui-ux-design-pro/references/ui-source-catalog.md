## Selection Recipes

- **iOS subscription app**: Apple HIG + Mobbin or Page Flows + Screenlane + WCAG for web companion screens.
- **B2B SaaS dashboard**: Atlassian or Carbon + SaaSFrame + Refero + WCAG.
- **E-commerce storefront**: Polaris + a real checkout flow source + Land-book for marketing composition + WCAG.
- **Developer tool**: Primer + Fluent or Atlassian + SaaSFrame or Refero + WCAG.
- **Public form or booking flow**: GOV.UK + Page Flows + WCAG; prioritize task completion over visual novelty.

## Domain Search Recipes

Use these only when the catalog does not cover the task's risk or workflow. Select two current, first-party product surfaces at task time; do not turn this list into a static collection of screenshots.

- **Finance or trading**: Pair a regulated, real-time transaction surface with a decision-support surface. Check data freshness, confirmation, risk disclosure, and the separation of research from execution.
- **Healthcare**: Pair a care-seeking or appointment flow with a records or results flow. Check privacy cues, plain language, accessibility, consent, and error recovery.
- **Travel or booking**: Pair search and comparison with an itinerary or change flow. Check date and price transparency, availability changes, and recovery from invalid selections.
- **Operations or logistics**: Pair a monitoring dashboard with an exception-resolution flow. Check alert priority, map or table density, ownership, and time-sensitive escalation.
- **Learning, media, or habit product**: Pair discovery or onboarding with progress and resumption. Check motivation without coercion, content state, interruptions, and subscription boundaries.
